## Editorial integrity

Editorial articles are governed by `EDITORIAL-GENUINE-SOURCE-POLICY.md` and `data/editorial_policy.json`.

The non-negotiable rule is: **AI is the linguistic editor, not the author or source.** Every substantive article paragraph must be traceable to approved genuine source material, with 100% source coverage, zero AI-original substantive content, verified quotations, complete provenance, and duplicate checks. The strict validator is `scripts/editorial_integrity.py`.
