# Site-wide public content policy

This policy applies to every public section, language, article, biography, story, quotation, caption, note, comment, summary, children item, media description, and interface copy that makes a factual or interpretive claim.

## Publication rule

1. Public substantive text must come from resources registered in the site library/corpus.
2. Linguistic revision, abridgement, translation, age adaptation, and re-ordering are allowed only when they do not introduce new facts, ideas, quotations, judgments, or events.
3. Every substantive paragraph or story scene must retain resource provenance through `sourceRefs`, `sources`, or the established editorial provenance fields.
4. Quotations must preserve the wording of the cited resource and carry a verified source reference. Named sayings of the Prophet, companions, followers, and followers of followers must preserve the named attribution supported by the source.
5. If provenance is missing or verification fails, the item remains draft/blocked and is not publishable.
6. Visual assets may illustrate sourced content but may not introduce new factual claims. Public pages must not contain implementation, generation, prompt, review, model, workflow, placeholder, or production-process commentary.
7. Arabic, English, and French versions must derive from the same source record; translation must not add content.
8. Existing unsourced material is retained only as migration material until rebuilt from registered resources; it must not be used as the basis for new publication.

## Public-language rule

Do not expose production notes or process labels such as generation notices, model references, prompt instructions, review notes, staging labels, implementation comments, or similar internal wording. Public copy must read as finished editorial content.

## Required provenance

A publishable record must have:
- one or more registered resource references;
- source coverage of the complete substantive text;
- verified quotations when quotations are present;
- no unsupported factual paragraph or scene;
- a passed provenance status.

This rule supersedes earlier permissions to publish unsourced original story or article text.
