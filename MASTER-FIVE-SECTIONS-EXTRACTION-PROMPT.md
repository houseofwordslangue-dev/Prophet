# MASTER PROMPT — Five Muhammad ﷺ Sections

Classification is exclusive by the **dominant main idea/object**, never by incidental keyword occurrence.

## النور — The Light
Publish here only material whose principal object is **الحقيقة المحمدية** or **النور المحمدي**. Generic discussion of light, guidance, illumination or Qur'anic uses of نور is not sufficient unless Muhammad ﷺ / the Muhammadan Reality is the dominant object.

## النبي — The Prophet
Publish here only material whose principal object is **النبي** or **النبوة**: prophecy, prophethood, revelation as constitutive of prophethood, signs/proofs of prophethood, and closely related material where the prophetic office is the main subject.

## الرسول — The Messenger
Publish here only material whose principal object is **الرسول** or **الرسالة**: mission, message, proclamation, conveying revelation, calling, warning, teaching as messenger, and the work/experience of delivering the message where الرسالة is dominant.

## الإنسان — The Human Being
Publish here only material principally about the lived human/personal experience of Muhammad ﷺ, including **الحياة اليومية لمحمد ﷺ**, **المشاكل الزوجية لمحمد ﷺ**, **قصص محمد ﷺ مع الصحابة**, **معاناة محمد ﷺ في تبليغ الدعوة**, and **القصص الشخصية لمحمد ﷺ**. Mere mention of an event or companion is insufficient unless the human/personal experience is the dominant object.

## الرحمة العظمى — The Great Mercy
Publish here only material whose principal object is **رحمة محمد ﷺ**, **الرحمة**, or **الرحمة العظمى**, including compassion, forgiveness, gentleness, clemency and merciful treatment where mercy is the dominant subject.

## Mandatory gates
- One primary section only; cross-links may point to other relevant sections.
- Do not force ambiguous material into any of the five sections merely to fill a quota.
- Existing old labels are non-authoritative and must be re-audited under this taxonomy.
- Source provenance must be preserved.
- No invented historical facts, quotations, dialogue, events or theological claims.
- Source-derived articles may be extracted, transcribed, translated, organized or linguistically revised without adding unsupported substantive content.
- Public-domain/reusable source text may be reproduced under its applicable rights. Protected modern editorial additions must not be copied merely because the underlying classical work is old.
- Publication target for this build: **100 articles in each section = 500 total**, each containing at least **1,000 source-derived words**, 100% provenance, no duplicate article bodies, and 0% AI-original substantive historical content.
