MASTER OVERRIDING INSTRUCTION
PROPHET MUHAMMAD ﷺ — COMPLETE SITE GOVERNANCE, CONTENT, TAXONOMY, RESEARCH, MEDIA, OCR, AUTOMATION AND PUBLICATION SYSTEM

STATUS

This instruction is the controlling master instruction for the entire Prophet Muhammad ﷺ website.

It:

- supersedes all earlier prompts where they conflict with this document;
- preserves earlier requirements that are compatible with it;
- consolidates the complete site vision into one implementation standard;
- governs all existing and future content, code, ingestion, automation, OCR, biographies, stories, books, media, research, localization, search, indexing and publication;
- applies to all repository branches, deployment environments, connected storage, imported datasets and future automated processes.

When an earlier instruction conflicts with this master instruction:

THIS MASTER INSTRUCTION PREVAILS.

Do not delete useful existing work merely because its original implementation predates this prompt.

Repair, migrate, classify and integrate it into the architecture defined below.


==================================================
1. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Build and continuously develop a comprehensive, professional, multilingual digital knowledge environment centered on the life, character, family, message, historical context, reception and legacy of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

The site shall serve:

- general readers;
- children and young people;
- students;
- researchers;
- historians;
- readers of Arabic, English and French;
- people from different cultural and religious backgrounds.

The public presentation must remain human-facing, dignified, scholarly, accessible and non-sectarian.

Do not use denominational or sectarian labels as public framing unless historically necessary inside a cited scholarly discussion.

The site should present the material itself rather than organize the public experience around sectarian identity.


==================================================
2. HIGHEST-PRIORITY GOVERNANCE RULE
==================================================

Historical truth and source integrity override:

- article quotas;
- publication quotas;
- minimum word-count targets;
- completeness percentages;
- automation speed;
- SEO goals;
- design convenience;
- content-generation targets.

Never invent historical facts merely to complete a page.

Never fabricate:

- quotations;
- hadith;
- historical events;
- dates;
- relationships;
- names;
- chains of transmission;
- source references;
- page numbers;
- book metadata;
- scholarly opinions;
- historical dialogue.

Where evidence is limited, publish less rather than manufacture more.


==================================================
3. CONTENT PROVENANCE CLASSES
==================================================

Every content item must have an internal provenance class.

Use at least:

A. SOURCED_EXTRACTED

Material directly:

- extracted from a source;
- OCRed from a source;
- transcribed from audio/video;
- translated from a source;
- quoted from a source;
- restored from corrupted OCR.

B. SOURCE_GROUNDED_SYNTHESIS

New editorial prose composed from identified sources.

All substantive historical assertions must be traceable to the underlying sources.

C. LEGACY_UNSOURCED_STORY

Existing unsourced story material already present in the project.

Preserve it unless it creates a serious factual, legal or technical problem.

D. GENERATED_UNSOURCED_STORY

New storytelling material automatically generated as part of the site's story corpus.

It must remain internally distinguishable from sourced historical material.

Never assign a historical source citation to generated content unless the specific claim genuinely comes from that source.

E. REVIEW_REQUIRED

Material that cannot currently be verified, reconstructed or confidently classified.

These classes must never be silently collapsed into one another.


==================================================
4. SOURCED AND UNSOURCED STORY CORPORA
==================================================

Maintain both:

- sourced story corpora;
- unsourced/generated story corpora.

The sourced corpus should be expanded from verified books, articles, manuscripts, research, lectures, transcripts, audio and video resources.

The existing unsourced corpus must be preserved.

Similar unsourced story material may continue to be generated automatically.

However:

- sourced and unsourced/generated corpora must remain clearly distinguished internally;
- generated stories must never be falsely represented as historical quotations or documentary evidence;
- factual historical assertions contained inside generated stories should, where possible, remain consistent with verified history;
- storytelling invention must not overwrite or alter canonical historical records.

For child-directed generated stories, age-appropriate literary adaptation is permitted, but invented dialogue or scene-setting must not be presented as documented historical quotation.


==================================================
5. CANONICAL INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

Use one stable information architecture.

Major canonical areas include:

- النور
- النبي
- الرسول
- الإنسان
- الرحمة العظمى
- الأسرة النبوية
- الصحابة
- السير والتراجم
- أحباب الله
- المصادر والدراسات
- الوسائط
- المقالات والموضوعات
- search/research interfaces where appropriate

Do not create parallel, competing taxonomies for the same material.

Earlier conceptual taxonomies may survive as:

- tags;
- thematic collections;
- filters;
- article series;
- internal metadata;

but must not create duplicate top-level architectures unless intentionally approved.


==================================================
6. أحباب الله — CHILDREN'S SECTION
==================================================

أحباب الله is the canonical destination for ALL material specifically designed for children or young people.

This includes:

- illustrated stories;
- animated stories;
- very short stories;
- child-friendly Seerah stories;
- child biographies;
- educational videos;
- cartoons;
- audio;
- nasheeds;
- Qur'an learning;
- duʿāʾ learning;
- Arabic learning;
- manners and character education;
- quizzes;
- family-oriented educational activities.

Examples of child media sources already requested include:

- Omar & Hana;
- One4Kids / Zaky;
- Muslim Kids TV;

and other suitable child-directed media.

All such child material belongs inside أحباب الله even when the subject is:

- the Prophet ﷺ;
- his family;
- companions;
- followers;
- Qur'an;
- prayer;
- ethics.

However, the scholarly biography of a person remains in السير والتراجم.

Child content may link to canonical biography pages but must not replace them.

Recommended internal children taxonomy:

CONTENT TYPE
- verified readings
- illustrated stories
- very short stories
- animated stories
- video
- audio
- interactive learning

SUBJECT
- السيرة والنبي ﷺ
- القرآن والحفظ
- الدعاء والذكر
- الصلاة والعبادات
- الآداب والأخلاق
- الإيمان
- العربية والتعلم
- الأناشيد والمديح
- رمضان والمناسبات
- الأسرة والحياة اليومية
- القصص والقيم
- المعرفة والتعليم

AGE
Use structured age bands as appropriate.


==================================================
7. السير والتراجم — CANONICAL BIOGRAPHY SYSTEM
==================================================

السير والتراجم is an independent primary menu section.

It must never be nested under أحباب الله.

Apply permanently:

ONE PERSON = ONE CANONICAL BIOGRAPHY PAGE.

The canonical biography system must include, where relevant:

- ancestors;
- grandparents;
- parents;
- foster relations;
- spouses;
- children;
- grandchildren;
- great-grandchildren;
- wider family;
- companions;
- narrators;
- tabiʿin;
- atbaʿ al-tabiʿin;
- scholars and historical figures relevant to the site's corpus.

Groups such as:

- family;
- companions;
- narrators;
- followers;
- ancestors;

are filters, collections or entry points into the canonical biography system.

They must not create competing biography pages for the same person.

The old standalone narrator model must remain only as compatibility routing if old URLs exist.

Narrator biographies belong inside السير والتراجم.


==================================================
8. EXACT-PERSON OWNERSHIP
==================================================

Every biographical passage must be assigned according to its actual subject.

A passage about Fatima does not become Ali material merely because Ali is mentioned.

A passage about Ali does not become Fatima material merely because Fatima is mentioned.

A multi-person passage must be classified according to:

- primary subject;
- event context;
- actual narrative focus.

Secondary persons should be linked rather than receiving duplicated biography text.

Biographical material belongs in the relevant canonical person page.

Distinct thematic material may remain a separate article while linking to all relevant people.


==================================================
9. SPECIAL BIOGRAPHY PAGES
==================================================

Maintain specially designed canonical pages for:

- Sayyidina Ali;
- Sayyidatina Fatima al-Zahra.

These may use distinctive:

- typography;
- visual composition;
- calligraphic treatment;
- historical structure;
- relationship navigation;
- story presentation;

while remaining inside the same canonical biography system.

Do not create duplicate alternative biographies.


==================================================
10. BIOGRAPHY DEPTH
==================================================

Build professional researcher-level biographies containing, when reliable material exists:

- names and variants;
- lineage;
- birth;
- family;
- historical setting;
- relationship to the Prophet ﷺ;
- major events;
- journeys;
- battles where relevant;
- transmitted reports;
- sayings;
- historical stories;
- teachers;
- students;
- narrations;
- later reception;
- death;
- burial where established;
- source notes;
- related media;
- related books;
- related articles.

Earlier targets such as 5,000 words are enrichment goals, NOT authorization to pad content.

If the evidence supports only a shorter biography, keep it shorter.

Never manufacture length.


==================================================
11. FAMILY AND LINEAGE
==================================================

Maintain comprehensive coverage of:

- ancestors up to Adnan;
- grandparents;
- parents;
- spouses;
- children;
- grandchildren;
- great-grandchildren;
- relevant descendants;
- family relationships.

Maintain an interactive lineage/family-tree experience where useful.

Each person in the tree should resolve to the canonical biography when a biography exists.


==================================================
12. COMPANIONS, FOLLOWERS AND NARRATORS
==================================================

Seek the widest responsibly sourced coverage of:

- companions;
- tabiʿin;
- atbaʿ al-tabiʿin;
- narrators.

Do not treat large numerical targets such as 120,000 companions as permission to manufacture identities or biographies.

Large corpus targets are acquisition targets only.

Every person record must have:

- source identity;
- name normalization;
- deduplication;
- canonical ID;
- generation/group metadata where available;
- source provenance.


==================================================
13. PROPHET'S NAMES
==================================================

Maintain the requested large collection of names/titles associated with the Prophet ﷺ, including the target collection of 201 names where supported by the project's referenced tradition and resources.

For each entry, where available provide:

- name;
- linguistic meaning;
- source;
- context;
- related narration;
- related story/event;
- scholarly note where needed.

Do not create unsupported prophetic names merely to reach a numeric target.


==================================================
14. آثار AND HISTORICAL PLACES/OBJECTS
==================================================

Maintain a structured collection of آثار associated with the Prophet ﷺ around the world.

For each item include, where possible:

- name;
- location;
- description;
- historical claim;
- provenance of the claim;
- scholarly/historical status;
- imagery;
- related sources.

Clearly distinguish:

- well-attested historical material;
- traditional attribution;
- later attribution;
- uncertain attribution.

AI-generated illustrative imagery may be used where allowed by editorial policy, but must never be represented internally as documentary evidence.


==================================================
15. QUOTATIONS AND SAYINGS
==================================================

For sayings attributed to:

- the Prophet ﷺ;
- companions;
- tabiʿin;
- atbaʿ al-tabiʿin;

the speaker/author must be clearly stated.

Preserve wording faithfully when quoting.

For sayings from later persons, follow the existing editorial display rule if the public interface intentionally leaves the speaker unattributed.

However, internal provenance must still preserve the actual source when known.

Public attribution policy and internal source provenance are separate concepts.

Never fabricate a saying.


==================================================
16. ARTICLE POLICY
==================================================

Maintain 2 principal scholarly article modes:

A. EXTRACTED / ADAPTED SOURCE ARTICLE

Based substantially on:

- book passages;
- research;
- lecture transcription;
- audio transcription;
- video transcription;
- historical records;
- OCR-restored text.

AI may perform:

- proofreading;
- organization;
- headings;
- punctuation;
- translation;
- linguistic cleanup;
- non-substantive transitions.

B. SOURCE-GROUNDED SYNTHESIS ARTICLE

AI may compose an article using multiple identified sources.

The article must contain no substantive historical assertions unsupported by the source corpus.

Do not label extracted source articles as AI-generated merely because AI corrected language.

Do not present source-grounded synthesis as a verbatim source extraction.


==================================================
17. THEMATIC ARTICLES VS BIOGRAPHIES
==================================================

A thematic article remains thematic when the main subject is:

- an event;
- moral theme;
- relationship;
- battle;
- historical episode;
- teaching;
- social theme;
- family theme;
- mercy;
- human character;
- message;
- spirituality.

Mentioning a person does not automatically convert an article into that person's biography.

However, person-specific biography facts discovered in thematic research should enrich that person's canonical biography where appropriate.
