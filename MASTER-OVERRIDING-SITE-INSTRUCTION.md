STATUS

This instruction is the controlling master instruction for the entire Prophet Muhammad ﷺ website.

It:

- supersedes all earlier prompts where they conflict with this document;
- preserves earlier requirements that are compatible with it;
- consolidates the complete site vision into one implementation standard;
- governs all existing and future content, code, ingestion, automation, OCR, biographies, stories, books, media, research, localization, search, indexing and publication;

THIS MASTER INSTRUCTION PREVAILS.

==================================================
1. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Build and continuously develop a comprehensive, professional, multilingual digital knowledge environment centered on the life, character, family, message, historical context, reception and legacy of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

The site shall serve:

- general readers;
- children and young people;
- students;
- researchers;
- historians;
- readers of Arabic, English and French;
- people from different cultural and religious backgrounds.

The public presentation must remain human-facing, dignified, scholarly, accessible and non-sectarian.

Do not use denominational or sectarian labels as public framing unless historically necessary inside a cited scholarly discussion.

The site should present the material itself rather than organize the public experience around sectarian identity.


==================================================

==================================================

Historical truth and source integrity override:

- publication quotas;

==================================================
3. CONTENT PROVENANCE CLASSES
==================================================

Every content item must have an internal provenance class.

Use at least:

A. SOURCED_EXTRACTED

Material directly:

- extracted from a source;
- OCRed from a source;
- transcribed from audio/video;
- translated from a source;
- quoted from a source;
- restored from corrupted OCR.

B. SOURCE_GROUNDED_SYNTHESIS

New editorial prose composed from identified sources.

All substantive historical assertions must be traceable to the underlying sources.

C. LEGACY_UNSOURCED_STORY

Existing unsourced story material already present in the project.

Preserve it unless it creates a serious factual, legal or technical problem.

D. GENERATED_UNSOURCED_STORY

New storytelling material automatically generated as part of the site's story corpus.

It must remain internally distinguishable from sourced historical material.

Never assign a historical source citation to generated content unless the specific claim genuinely comes from that source.

E. REVIEW_REQUIRED

Material that cannot currently be verified, reconstructed or confidently classified.

These classes must never be silently collapsed into one another.

==================================================

- sourced story corpora;

20. OCR SOURCE PRIORITY
==================================================

Use this recovery priority:

1. Exact original scan/page.
2. Another edition/copy of the same work in Google Drive.
3. Another edition/copy in GitHub.
4. EPUB/native-text version.
5. Other verified copies in the site library.
6. Corresponding passages in authoritative listed resources.
7. Other legally usable/public/open copies when needed.

Prefer comparison of several witnesses whenever possible.


==================================================
21. PERMITTED OCR REPAIR
==================================================

AI may:

- restore spaces;
- repair obvious OCR substitutions;
- restore strongly supported missing letters;
- reconstruct broken words;
- remove duplicated OCR noise;
- normalize Unicode;
- restore sentence/paragraph boundaries;
- restore punctuation;
- reorder lines when page layout clearly proves the order;
- remove page numbers/headers/footers accidentally inserted;
- join page-crossing fragments;
- correct obvious typographical OCR errors;
- perform necessary linguistic proofreading.

AI must NOT:

- invent historical information;
- rewrite an author's substantive wording;
- paraphrase genuine quotations merely for elegance;
- silently merge contradictory readings;
- manufacture text where witnesses do not support reconstruction.


==================================================
22. OCR CROSS-VERIFICATION
==================================================

For every repaired passage:

- identify person/work/article;
- retain original corrupted extraction;
- identify page/source;
- locate corroborating witnesses;
- compare variants;
- choose best-supported reading;
- retain consulted sources;
- retain original OCR separately;
- retain corrected OCR;
- calculate verification/confidence status.

Historical spelling should be preserved when meaningful.
